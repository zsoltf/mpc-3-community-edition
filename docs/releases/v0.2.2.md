# v0.2.2

**[Build your image](https://zsoltf.github.io/mpc-3-community-edition/)**
| **[Controller guide](https://zsoltf.github.io/mpc-3-community-edition/guide.html)**

## Fixed

- The X-Touch no longer freezes when automation Write is armed and you move a
  plugin parameter, mute, solo, a pad or a program control.
- Track names and colors no longer turn into dashes after several project
  loads.

## Improved

- Plugin parameters respond about twice as fast from the X-Touch.
- Less background work. Meters stop sending when the audio is silent.

## Known limits

- Recorded automation is stepped at about ten points per second.
- The center button as Enter and one-press Duplicate Sequence are not enabled.
- A fast wheel spin on some sliders overshoots.

## Upgrading

Flash the image as usual. Settings and projects are kept.
